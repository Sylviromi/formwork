# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release, for Forge 1.20.1.

- Place whole multiblocks from your inventory or a linked AE2 network, all or nothing.
- Generated Mekanism multiblocks: fission reactor, industrial turbine, thermoelectric boiler, induction matrix, dynamic tank, thermal evaporation plant.
- Choose every part per block, including Mekanism ports, valves and glass anywhere on the walls.
- More Thermal Evaporation tiers and Mekanism Extras induction tiers.
- Materials tab with inventory and ME counts. AE2 crafts every missing block as one job, planned in AE2's own screen.
- JEI: structures as recipes of their materials, with a preview.
- Placement card: anchor, move, turn, skip blocked spots.
- Edit a placed multiblock in place, or dismantle it and get the blocks back.
- Structures and variant sets come from datapacks (`data/<namespace>/formwork/...`).
